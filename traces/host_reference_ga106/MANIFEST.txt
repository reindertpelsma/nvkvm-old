MEASURED, and the reason this reference is worth having
  * 479 of 613 ioctls — 78% of the entire RM control plane for a compute program —
    belong to cuCtxCreate. The divergence surface for "stock driver -> first
    compute" is almost entirely inside that one call.
  * `ce` and `launch` are IDENTICAL: same 613 records, and after canonicalisation
    NOT ONE DIFFERING BYTE. Loading a PTX module, allocating two more buffers,
    launching a kernel and synchronising cost ZERO RM ioctls. The control plane
    genuinely ends at cuCtxCreate; everything after is doorbell/pushbuffer traffic
    that no ioctl trace on either side can see.
  * First ioctl of cuCtxCreate on this host: nvidiactl RM_CONTROL cmd=0x20803601,
    at index 129 (see nvd_selftest.sh's positive control).

NOISE FLOOR (host vs host, same binary, 12 pairings across 6 stages)
  opcode alignment      exact — equal record counts, ratio 1.000000, zero MISSING/EXTRA
  declared paramsSize   identical in every record
  rc / NV status        identical in every record
  differing bytes       100% explained: ~1166 handle-symbol, ~672 pointer-symbol,
                        ~150 "addrlike" (both sides a plausible 64-bit address in the
                        same slot — spot-checked and they are stack/heap pointers such
                        as 0x7ffd08143e8c vs 0x7ffe5c2fa97c, i.e. ASLR)
  UNEXPLAINED           ZERO, in all 12 pairings

⊘ WHAT THIS ORACLE CANNOT SEE — read before drawing a conclusion from a green diff
  1. It is an ioctl/mmap trace. Everything after cuCtxCreate is invisible to it (see
     the ce/launch identity above), so a green diff says NOTHING about the doorbell,
     pushbuffer, semaphore or completion planes. It bounds the CONTROL plane only.
  2. It records the userspace boundary. Whatever the guest's kernel driver and the
     GSP do between the ioctl and the hardware is not here.
  3. It is a HOST reference. It says what a real driver on real hardware does; it
     does not say what our emulation is permitted to do differently and still be
     correct. A divergence is a lead, not automatically a defect.
  4. The GSP RPC layer is NOT captured (see the scoping note in the harness).
