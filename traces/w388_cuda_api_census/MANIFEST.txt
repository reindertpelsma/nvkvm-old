WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
  NVOS46_FLAGS_DEFER_TLB_INVALIDATION is a flag on an ioctl that CUDA never issues.
  ⇒ The DEFER half of w387's silent conjunction is NOT on the application path. It is a
    hostile-guest / raw-client concern and gates neither phase 1 nor phase 2.
  ⇒ libcuda maps through UVM. The coverage question moves to UVM, which is uncovered for
    ENTIRELY DIFFERENT reasons (own page tables, PTE writes by CPU store or CE copy,
    invalidates as pushbuffer methods).

⊘ WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH — stated, not buried
  - `peer` is UNMEASURED, not zero. It needs a 2-GPU rental.
  - GRAPHICS (libGL/EGL) and NVENC/NVDEC are NOT covered. They do not go through UVM and
    are the classic RM DMA-mapping clients — plausibly the reason this escape exists.
  - One arch (GA106), two drivers (560, 580). Not a statement about all hardware.
  - ⊘ A PREDICTION OF MINE DIED HERE: I ranked the CUDA VMM API as most likely to issue
    0x57, on the reasoning that client-managed explicit mapping is exactly what
    NV04_MAP_MEMORY_DMA provides. It issues ZERO. cuMemMap goes through UVM like the rest.
